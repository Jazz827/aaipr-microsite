Project Scope

What this microsite covers, what it doesn't, and who it's written for.

Home/Project Scope
1Purpose

A research microsite, not a legal service

This is a research microsite examining data governance, infrastructure, and environmental planning in Africa. It exists to make a genuinely cross-disciplinary picture — law, physical infrastructure, and sustainability — accessible in one place, in plain language, with sources attached.

It is not legal advice, and it is not a comprehensive database of every African country's data protection regime. Where specific laws are described, treat them as a starting orientation — always confirm current text with the relevant national authority.

2Scope

What's in, and what's out

In scope

Cross-border personal data transfer law in a representative set of African countries; the physical infrastructure that supports data flows; African Union harmonisation efforts; environmental site-selection and sustainability practices for data centres.

Out of scope

Country-by-country legal advice, exhaustive coverage of all 55 African Union member states, non-personal or purely commercial data regulation, and detailed engineering specifications for data-centre construction.

3Audience

Who this is written for

Students, researchers, professionals, and policy-aware readers who want a clear, well-sourced entry point into African digital governance — without needing a background in law, infrastructure engineering, or environmental science to follow it.

4Approach

How this was put together

Content is drawn from primary sources — national legislation, African Union documents, and industry and policy research — listed in full on the References page. Legal and technical terms are defined in plain language in the Glossary rather than assumed knowledge.
