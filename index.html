Cross-Border Data Transfers & African Data Infrastructure

How Nigeria and Kenya show the link between data law and the physical systems that make data move.

Data crosses borders every day through cloud platforms, AI tools, banking systems, and research networks. This microsite explores cross-border data transfers, the data infrastructure that makes them possible — from data centres to fibre networks and cooling constraints — and what both mean for digital sovereignty in Nigeria and Kenya.
