Africa's Data Centres, Site Selection & Cross-Border Data Transfers

A digital infrastructure explainer for policymakers, investors, developers, and researchers — with a regional African perspective.

This site explains why data centres matter in Africa, how to evaluate a viable site, how cross-border data transfer rules shape investment decisions, and what realistic, phased recommendations look like for the road ahead.

Three Themes

Infrastructure, policy, and investment

Data centres sit at the intersection of three things that are usually discussed separately: the physical infrastructure needed to build them, the cross-border data policy that shapes how they're used, and the investment decisions that determine where they get built at all. This site treats them as one connected picture.
