Cross-Border Data Transfers & African Data Infrastructure

A research microsite on the legal, infrastructural, and environmental dimensions of data in Africa.

Data crosses borders every day through cloud platforms, AI tools, banking systems, and research networks. This site explores cross-border data transfers across African countries, the data infrastructure that makes them possible, and the environmental planning decisions that shape where that infrastructure can responsibly be built.
