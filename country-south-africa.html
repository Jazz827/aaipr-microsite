1Market Maturity

Where the market stands today

South Africa is the continent's most developed data centre ecosystem, anchored by Teraco — the continent's most carrier-dense colocation operator — alongside a major Vantage Data Centers campus in Johannesburg. It's the benchmark other African markets are measured against, though it's now approaching capacity limits as power and infrastructure constraints intensify.

2Infrastructure Readiness

Power, fibre, and physical constraints

Infrastructure here is the most mature on the continent: stronger grid reliability than any other African market, established fibre routes, and a deep base of technical skills. Even so, national power constraints are a live concern, and new capacity increasingly has to plan around energy availability rather than assume it.

3Policy & Regulatory Context

The legal environment

Regulated under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), which — unlike most peer laws — extends data protection to companies and trusts as well as individuals, and permits cross-border transfer only where the recipient offers adequate protection. A 2024 National Data and Cloud Policy separately requires certain government data to stay within South Africa's borders.

4Demand Drivers

What's fuelling growth

Demand is driven by a large domestic enterprise base, the arrival of global hyperscalers seeking a African foothold, and South Africa's role as a financial and business hub for the wider Southern African region.

5Connectivity

Fibre and network access

The most carrier-dense connectivity market in Africa, with multiple submarine cable landing points and well-established internet exchange points, particularly around Johannesburg and Cape Town.

6Investment Relevance

What this means for investors and developers

The lowest-risk entry point into the African market for hyperscale and colocation investment, given proven demand and infrastructure — but also the market where land, power, and expansion headroom are becoming genuinely scarce, making site selection more competitive than elsewhere on the continent.
