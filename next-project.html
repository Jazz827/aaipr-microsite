From two countries to a continental picture

The Nigeria vs Kenya comparison on this microsite is a starting point, not the finish line. The next phase widens the lens.

1

Expand the country comparison

Add more African markets to the analysis — building out a fuller picture of how cross-border data transfer rules vary across the continent.

2

Examine regional policy alignment

Look at how far individual national laws align — or diverge — from continental frameworks like the African Union's data policy efforts, and what that means for cross-border commerce.

3

Map infrastructure conditions

Go deeper on the data infrastructure conditions needed to support secure, affordable, and efficient cross-border data flows at scale.

Why this matters

Africa's digital future depends on both trust and infrastructure. Data laws create the rules — data centres and networks make those rules workable. Understanding both, across more than two countries, is the only way to see where the continent is actually headed.
